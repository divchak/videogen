📖 **About the Song:** 
Thiruppalliyezhuchi ("Sacred Awakening") is part of the Nalayira Divya Prabandham, the holiest Vaishnava canon in Tamil literature. It is sung daily at dawn during the Thiruvaaradhana (morning worship) at the Srirangam Ranganathaswamy temple. The composer, Thondaradipodi Alvar, whose name translates to "dust on the feet of devotees," represents the absolute pinnacle of humility and devotion.
