> 📜 **SONG MEANING (Overview — for reference):**
> *'பொன்னார் மேனியனே' ('O Golden-Bodied One') is Sundarar's opening masterpiece — the very first Pathigam of the 7th Tirumurai. In this hymn, Sundarar paints the most vivid portrait of Shiva: his golden body, tiger skin tied at the waist, lightning-red matted locks adorned with shining Konrai flowers. He addresses Shiva as 'Manne' (O King), 'Maamaniye' (O Great Gem), 'Maanikkame' (O Ruby). The refrain 'Anne Ennaiyaal Vantharulai Aaruraare' — 'O Mother! Come and grace me, O Arurar!' — captures Sundarar's unique intimacy: calling the all-powerful Shiva 'Mother' (Anne) and sharing the name 'Arurar' with the Lord, proclaiming poet and deity as one. The temple is Thiruvarur — the Prithvi Sabha where Shiva dances the Bhoomi Natana.*
> — Sundarar, Thevaram 7th Tirumurai | Temple: Thiruvarur Thyagaraja Temple | Raga: Shankarabharanam
