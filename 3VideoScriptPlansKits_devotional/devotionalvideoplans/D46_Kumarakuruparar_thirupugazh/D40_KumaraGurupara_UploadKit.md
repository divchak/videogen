"குமர குருபர" is a Thirupugazh composed by the Tamil Siddhar-poet
**Arunagirinathar** (15th century CE) in praise of Lord Murugan as
**Swaminathaswamy** — worshipped at **Swamimalai**, one of the six sacred
Arupadai Veedu abodes of Murugan near Kumbakonam. This hymn celebrates the
most extraordinary legend in all of Saiva-Kaumara theology: the eternal
child Murugan personally teaching his own father Shiva the meaning of the
sacred Panchakshara mantra "சிவய நம" — earning him the title "Guruguha,"
Guru even to the Guru of the universe! Watch the six sparks of his cosmic
birth, his decisive victory over the tyrant-asura Soorapadman, and the
tender, world-reversing moment a father becomes student to his own son —
all rendered in stunning hyper-realistic 4K cinematic visuals!

📖 A NOTE ON THIS SONG'S IDENTITY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
This hymn is correctly a **Thirupugazh** (not a Thevaram) — identifiable by
its signature rhythmic chandam opening ("தனன தனதன தனன தனதன") and its
closing refrain "பெருமாளே," the personal poetic signature Arunagirinathar
placed at the end of all his 16,000+ Thirupugazh compositions. We believe
in presenting these sacred texts with full scholarly accuracy.

📖 ABOUT THIS SONG
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
The song opens with a cascade of Murugan's sacred names — Kumara (eternal
youth), Gurupara (Supreme Preceptor), Saravana-born, Guha, Shanmukha
(six-faced), the younger brother of elephant-faced Ganesha. It then reveals
his most extraordinary title: "sweet child, Son of Shiva," who personally
taught his own father the meaning of "சிவய நம" — becoming "the jewel-Guru
who grants grace as preceptor of the preceptor." The poet cries out:
devotees tormented by the fruits of their own past karma beg for refuge —
"Do you not hear our cry of Abhaya?" The song then turns to epic battle:
Soorapadman once churned the seven seas and shattered the directions of
the world, until Mayil Veera (the Peacock-Warrior) struck him down. In a
moment of hushed wonder, the poet reminds us: this same child is Guru-Lord
even to Shiva — the god whose fire-anklet once conquered death itself,
whose matted locks bear the sacred Ganga — for even that cosmically
powerful father humbled himself to learn from his son. The song resolves
at Swamimalai — the lotus-beautiful Guru Hill — where the Lord rests in
eternal glory, praised by the Vedas themselves.

✨ THE MEANING:
🦚 "குமர குருபர" — Eternal Child, Supreme Preceptor
🕉️ "சிவய நம" — the sacred Panchakshara mantra Murugan taught his own father

🙏 "குருநாதா" — Guru-Lord, even to Shiva himself
