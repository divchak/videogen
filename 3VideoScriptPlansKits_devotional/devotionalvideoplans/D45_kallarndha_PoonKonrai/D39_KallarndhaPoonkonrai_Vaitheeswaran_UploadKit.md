📖 ABOUT THIS SONG
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
This Thevaram is part of the First Thirumurai, composed by Sambandar of Sirkazhi
(Kaundinya gotra). Each of its 11 verses ends with the refrain naming the sacred
site "புள்ளிருக்கு வேளூரே" — Thiruppullirukkuvelur, "the place where the bird
resides" — referring to the legendary eagle-brothers Jatayu and Sampaathi, who are
said to have attained liberation here after Jatayu's valiant fight with Ravana to
protect Sita. The final signature verse names Shiva as the one who "cures the
afflicted body and is himself the medicine for the disease of bad karma" — the
very reason this temple is worshipped today as Vaitheeswaran Koil, where devotees
still bring medicines to be blessed.

✨ THE MEANING:
🕉️ "கள்ளார்ந்த பூங்கொன்றை" — fragrant honey-filled konrai flowers in Shiva's hair
🔱 "புள்ளிருக்கு வேளூரே" — the sacred abode of the eagle-brothers Jatayu & Sampaathi
💊 "தீவினைக்கோர் மருந்தாவான்" — He Himself is the medicine for the disease of sin

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
