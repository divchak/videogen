## English Subtitles:


Can you believe that India's financial capital, Mumbai, was once given as a wedding dowry? Yes, it happened in 1661!

This agreement happened during the historic marriage alliance between King Charles II of England and Princess Catherine of Braganza, daughter of King John IV of Portugal

This royal marriage was a result of Portugal seeking England's help to face intense military pressure from Spain and the Dutch East India Company.

Signed on June 23, 1661, this dowry included Bombay, the port of Tangier in Morocco, and 2 million Portuguese crowns

But at that time, the Portuguese did not view Bombay as a high-value asset. It was just a small fishing and agricultural outpost across seven islands

King Charles II initially struggled to govern these far-flung islands, finding the administrative and military costs far too expensive

To offload the burden, on March 27, 1668, he signed a Royal Charter

Through this, he leased the islands of Bombay to the English East India Company. Do you know the annual rent?

An incredibly cheap annual rent of just £10 in gold! Those islands that changed hands so cheaply are today's massive Mumbai with towering skyscrapers!
